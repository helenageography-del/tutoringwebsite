About Helena

Helena is a British, Qualified Teacher Status (QTS) geography tutor with seven years of classroom experience across schools in the UK and internationally. She specialises in GCSE and A-Level Geography, with a particular focus on exam skills: helping students turn solid subject knowledge into strong, consistent exam performance. She has experience supporting students with additional learning needs, such as dyslexia, and is always happy to adapt her teaching strategies to suit the way each student learns best.
